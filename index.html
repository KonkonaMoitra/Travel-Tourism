Our Story

Our Journey
Born from a passion for exploration and a deep love for the diverse landscapes of India, EverWild began as a simple idea—to make travel planning easy, personal, and unforgettable. What started as a dream is now a growing platform that connects Travellers with authentic experiences across India, from the snow-clad peaks of Himachal to the serene backwaters of Kerala. Everwide was created with the belief that travel should be more than just moving from one place to another—it should be a story worth telling. Today, our website stands as a user-friendly, all-in-one travel companion, designed to help you discover hidden gems, plan meaningful journeys, and embrace the vibrant spirit of India with every step.
Meet The Founders
Everwild Travellers was born from a shared passion for discovery, nature, and meaningful travel. Originally founded in 2015 by three nature lovers in the heart of Himachal Pradesh, it began as a humble trekking guide service along the hidden Himalayan trails. Their dream was simple but powerful—to help people escape the ordinary and step into the wild, immersing themselves in untouched landscapes and authentic local cultures.
In 2018, the vision expanded when Aarav Mehta, an avid Traveller and passionate entrepreneur from India, joined forces with the founding team. With a strong academic background in tourism and years of hands-on experience exploring the diverse corners of the country, Aarav brought a deeper purpose to Everwild’s mission. He saw travel not just as movement, but as transformation—where each journey becomes a story, every place holds meaning, and every Traveller walks away changed.
Under his leadership, Everwild Travellers now bridges nature and culture, offering journeys that go beyond the expected—connecting people, places, and purpose through authentic adventures that inspire and transform.
Together, the original spirit of wilderness and Aarav’s vision of cultural depth continue to guide Everwild Travellers as a trusted companion for conscious explorers seeking to experience India in its truest form.
